# Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placement by contacting
support. After this window, the order may already be processing and cannot
be modified.

**Do you offer price matching?**
We do not currently offer price matching with other retailers.

**How do I track my order?**
Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number
and a link to the courier's tracking page.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and popular digital wallets.
We do not currently accept cash on delivery.

**How do I contact human support?**
If our support agent is unable to resolve your issue, you can request to
be connected with a human support representative at any point in the
conversation.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Gift wrapping is not currently available as a checkout option.
