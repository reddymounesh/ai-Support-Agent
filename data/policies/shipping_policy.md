# Shipping Policy

We offer standard and express shipping for all orders within the country.

Standard shipping typically takes 5 to 7 business days from the date the
order is placed. Express shipping typically takes 1 to 2 business days and
is available at an additional cost during checkout.

Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days. Orders placed on
weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Once an order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. Delivery
times may be affected by weather conditions, regional courier delays, or
incorrect address information provided at checkout.

We currently do not offer international shipping. All orders must be
delivered to an address within the country.

If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, please
contact support with your order ID so we can investigate with the courier.
