# Return Policy

You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a refund
or exchange. To start a return, contact support with your order ID and the
reason for the return.

To initiate a return:

1. Contact support with your order ID.
2. You will receive a return authorization and a prepaid shipping label
   for eligible returns.
3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging, if possible.
4. Drop the package off at the specified courier location.

Some items cannot be returned, including perishable goods, personalized or
custom-made products, and intimate or hygiene-sensitive items once opened.

Exchanges for a different size or color are subject to availability. If
the requested item is unavailable, a refund will be issued instead.

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For all other
returns, a small return shipping fee may be deducted from your refund.
