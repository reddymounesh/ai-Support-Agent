# Refund Policy

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. Once a
return is received and inspected, refunds are typically processed within
5 to 7 business days.

Items must be returned in their original condition, unused, and with all
original packaging and tags attached to qualify for a full refund. Items
that show signs of use, damage, or missing packaging may be eligible for
only a partial refund, at our discretion.

Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defective
or incorrect item sent by us.

Refunds are not available for digital products, gift cards, or items
marked as final sale at the time of purchase.

If a refund has not appeared in your account within 10 business days of
confirmation, please contact your bank or card provider, as processing
times can vary between financial institutions.
